Pocket Loops

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Pocket Loops is made by Sandybyte. For privacy questions or requests, email support@sandybyte.com.

Your game data

Your puzzles, garden, settings and difficulty observations are saved on your device. There is no Pocket Loops account, cloud sync or third-party gameplay analytics in this version. The game uses your play to select puzzles; revenue-based difficulty adjustment is currently disabled.

You can reset your difficulty profile in Settings without removing your garden or purchases. Removing the app removes its local data from your device, subject to copies in your device backups. Reinstalling may remove your garden.

Advertising and privacy choices

Pocket Loops is free to play with ads. If you are not a Garden Club member, the app uses Google AdMob to show rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example for an extra hint, extra moves or a bonus) and occasional full-screen ads between loops. Before ads are requested, Google's consent tool shows privacy choices where the law requires them, including in the UK, and you can accept, decline or manage your options.

When ads are shown, Google may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, crash and performance data, ads shown, and interactions such as video views. Google may use information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and improving its services, and may work with advertising partners. We request non-personalised ads; this does not mean that no information is processed. The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

You can change your choices at any time from Advertising privacy choices in Settings when that option applies. Garden Club removes all ads while your membership is active. See how Google uses information from apps using its services and Google's advertising information for details and controls.

When live ads report a value, the app may record the amount, currency and precision in a local estimate on your device. The app does not send your puzzle performance or this estimate to Google.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device (for example when your daily loop or garden harvest is ready). They are created locally; no notification data is sent to us or to third parties. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.

Purchases

Apple processes Garden Club subscriptions, sun pack purchases and payments. The app checks verified StoreKit transactions to determine your membership and to add purchased suns; it does not receive your payment card details. Suns are stored on your device. Apple handles purchase records under Apple's privacy policy.

Contacting us and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include so we can respond. Please do not send sensitive information. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices and rights

You can review or change advertising privacy choices, turn reminders on or off, reset the local difficulty profile or delete local app data as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, access, correct or delete personal information held about you. Contact us at the address above; you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date at the top tells you when it was last updated.
