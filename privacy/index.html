Pocket Loops

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Pocket Loops is made by Sandybyte. For privacy questions or requests, email support@sandybyte.com.

Your game data

Your puzzles, garden, settings and difficulty observations are saved on your device. There is no Pocket Loops account, cloud sync or third-party gameplay analytics in this version. The game uses your play to select puzzles; revenue-based difficulty adjustment is currently disabled.

You can reset your difficulty profile in Settings without removing your garden or purchases. Removing the app removes its local data from your device, subject to copies in your device backups. Reinstalling may remove your garden.

Optional videos and privacy choices

Reward videos are off by default. If you confirm you are an adult and enable them, the app contacts Google's consent service and AdMob. Google may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, crash and performance data, ads shown, and interactions such as video views. Google may use information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and improving its services, and may work with advertising partners. We request non-personalised ads; this does not mean that no information is processed.

Where required, privacy choices are shown before an ad can be requested. You can turn videos off in Settings; that stops future ad requests and clears the local revenue profile. When required, Settings also offers advertising privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps using its services and Google's advertising information for details and controls.

When eligible live ads report a value, the app may record the amount, currency and precision in a local revenue estimate. The app does not send your puzzle performance or local estimate to Google. Test ads are excluded. The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Purchases

Apple processes Garden Club subscriptions and payments. The app checks verified StoreKit transactions to determine your membership; it does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles subscription records under Apple's privacy policy.

Contacting us and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include so we can respond. Please do not send sensitive information. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices and rights

You can control optional videos, review available advertising privacy choices, reset the local difficulty profile or delete local app data as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, access, correct or delete personal information held about you. Contact us at the address above; you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date at the top tells you when it was last updated.
