Pocket Loops

How can we help?

Questions, feedback or a puzzle that isn't behaving? We're here to help.

Contact Sandybyte

Email support@sandybyte.com. Please include your device, iOS version and a description of the problem. A screenshot can help; please leave out anything private.

How do I play?

Tap a tile to turn it. Join all the paths into one connected loop to finish the puzzle. Undo and hints are available when you need them.

Where is my garden saved?

Your garden and progress are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove that data. Your device backup settings may include app data; the game does not have its own cloud sync.

How do optional reward videos work?

Videos are off by default. An adult can choose to enable them in Settings. If a video is available, you may choose to watch it after a completed puzzle for an extra garden growth reward. Videos are never needed to keep playing. Turn them off at any time in Settings.

My Garden Club membership isn't showing

Check that you are signed in with the Apple Account used to subscribe, then open Settings in Pocket Loops and choose Restore Purchases. An internet connection may be needed. If it still isn't showing, contact us with the details above.

How do I manage or cancel Garden Club?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Garden Club. You can also use Manage Subscription in the app. See Apple's cancellation instructions.

How do I report an ad?

Open Settings in the app and choose Report an inappropriate ad for a link to Google's reporting form and any available recent ad details. Sending a report is your choice.
